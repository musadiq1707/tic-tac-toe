Play Tic Tac Toe online for free

Tic Tac Toe (also called Noughts and Crosses) is a classic two-player game on a 3×3 grid. Take turns placing X and O and be the first to line up three in a row — across, down or diagonally. This version runs instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop, with no download and no signup.

Game modes

Play vs Bot

Pick a difficulty, choose X or O, and decide who moves first.

  • Easy – mostly random moves, a gentle warm-up.
  • Medium – takes a win or blocks your threat when it can.
  • Hard – perfect play using minimax. The best you can do is draw.

2 Players

Share one screen with a friend, set both names, and keep a running score as you alternate who starts.

How to play

  1. Choose 2 Players or vs Bot in Game setup.
  2. Tap or click an empty square to place your mark.
  3. Get three of your marks in a row to win; if the board fills up, it's a draw.
  4. Use Undo to take back a move and Play Again for a new round.

Tic Tac Toe strategy tips

  • Take the centre square if it's free — it's part of four possible lines.
  • Corners beat edges: they give you more ways to build a double threat.
  • Always check whether your opponent has two in a row and block it.
  • Create a "fork" — two winning threats at once — so they can only block one.

Frequently asked questions

Can you beat the Hard bot in Tic Tac Toe?

No. The Hard bot plays perfectly using the minimax algorithm, so the best result you can get against it is a draw.

Who goes first in Tic Tac Toe?

By tradition X goes first. In this game you can choose who opens in bot mode (you, the bot, or alternating), and two-player rounds alternate the starting player.

Can I play Tic Tac Toe with a friend on the same device?

Yes. Choose 2 Players in Game setup, enter both names and take turns tapping or clicking squares on one screen.

Are my scores saved?

Scores are saved in your browser, separately for two-player mode and each bot difficulty. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes. Use the arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to place a mark, R to restart, M to toggle music and Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo.
